PocketSwing
Cricket

Your phone is the bat. Hold it like a bat with the screen facing the bowler, and swing.

Settings

The game works out which edge of the phone points down when you take your stance. If you hold it the other way round, it corrects the grip by itself after two swings.

How to play

  • Grip: hold the phone with both hands like a bat handle, one edge pointing down to the ground. The screen is the bat face, so it points at the bowler (your TV or screen). You can change this in Settings, and the game also corrects it by itself.
  • Turn on rotation lock so the screen doesn't flip while you swing.
  • Before each ball: stand side-on, ground the bat and hold still. The bowler runs in when you're steady.
  • Hit: swing quickly when the ball reaches your bat. A gentle push on time is a defensive block. Lift the bat back, then swing down through the ball to keep it on the ground. Swing up through the ball to hit it in the air.
  • Aim: the ball goes where your bat face points and where your swing travels. Swing toward the right of the screen to hit it right, and toward the left to hit it left. A flat, sideways swing plays a pull or a cut.
  • Footwork: step or move the phone left/right and your batsman moves across the crease. Move it forward for a front-foot shot and back for a back-foot shot. Get forward to full balls and back to short ones.
  • Timing: early sends the ball a little to the left side, late to the right. Badly mistimed shots edge to the keeper or slips.
  • TV: mirror your phone, pick "TV (mirrored)" in Settings, then run Calibrate timing. The game keeps fine-tuning your timing as you play.
  • Pause: hold the II button for 1 second.

Keyboard: hold ← / → (with ↑ or ↓ for straighter or finer) to aim · Space hit · E lofted shot · Q block · A / D step across · W front foot · S back foot · P pause.

Safety: clear about 2 m around you, away from people, pets, the TV and glass. Grip firmly — a phone case with a wrist strap is best. You don't need full power.
